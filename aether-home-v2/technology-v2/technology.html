APG · Autonomous Process Generator

APG, the engine that quotes and plans the part.

APG reads geometry straight out of your CAD file, plans the machining process behind it, and hands back a quote you can see the reasoning for — the same way, every time.

→Reads the drawing, not just the shape. ISO and ASME tolerances and finishes are picked up directly from the STEP file.
→Shows its work. Cost, cycle time, setup time and the toolpath behind them — not a number with no explanation.
→Re-plans in minutes. When machines, tooling or priorities change, the process updates instead of going back to a programmer.

FacOS · Factory Operating System

FacOS, the OS that runs the whole floor.

FacOS runs the floor in real time — machines, robots, orders and inspection on one model. Equipment that was never designed to talk to each other behaves like a single factory.

→Closed loop. Probe and CMM results correct tool offsets while the job is still running.
→Built for high mix. Universal fixturing and dynamic scheduling absorb thousands of part variants with little changeover.
→Nothing hidden. Every station, order and operator on one screen, live.

QMS · Quality & Metrology

QMS, the loop that tunes the next part.

Parts are inspected on Zeiss CMM and the result returns to FacOS and APG. Quality is not a gate at the end of the line — it is what tunes the next part.

→First article inspection. The CMM validates the first parts against GD&T; deviations update tool offsets automatically.
→In-process control. Statistical sampling through the run, charted live.
→Full traceability. Material lot, machine, tool history and inspection results stay attached to every part.
→Autonomous compensation. Under 15 µm, the cell probes, adjusts and re-cuts without an operator.
